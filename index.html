FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay and confirm my order? +

Pay via UPI to our registered number. After payment, send the screenshot along with your order details on WhatsApp. We'll confirm within 2 hours and begin work immediately.

Is confidentiality guaranteed? +

Absolutely. Your documents and personal information are kept strictly confidential. We never share, publish, or reuse any client content. All interactions are private.

What is the turnaround time for thesis assistance? +

Thesis assistance timelines depend on scope. A single chapter takes 5–10 days. A full thesis (5–6 chapters) typically takes 3–8 weeks. Express delivery is available for an additional charge.

Do you provide revisions? +

Yes! We provide unlimited free revisions within 7 days of delivery for writing services. For plagiarism and AI humanizer services, revision is provided if the output doesn't meet the stated requirements.

Which plagiarism checker do you use? +

We use Turnitin and iThenticate — the same tools used by most universities and journals. You'll receive the full similarity report in PDF format.

Can you help with journal paper submission? +

Yes, we assist with formatting, cover letter writing, and guidance on journal selection (Scopus, WoS, UGC-CARE). However, the actual submission must be done by the author.

Is there a refund policy? +

If work has not started, a full refund is provided. Once work begins, a partial refund (50%) is offered. After delivery, refunds are not applicable but free revisions are provided to meet your requirements.

How do I send my document? +

You can share a Google Drive or Dropbox link in the order form, or send your document directly to us via WhatsApp (+91 90186 20566) or email (scholaraid.help@gmail.com) after placing your order.
